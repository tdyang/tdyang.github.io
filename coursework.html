Graduate · MS Robotics Engineering (in progress, '27)
RBE 521

Legged Robotics

Foundations and principles of parallel manipulators and legged robots. Advanced spatial and 3D kinematics and dynamics, workspace analysis, inverse and forward kinematics, motion analysis and control, and gait and stability/balance analysis of legged robots — including quadrupeds, hexapods, and other multi-legged systems. Final project: design, analysis, and simulation of a custom legged or parallel robot.

Learning outcomes
  • Analyze 3D kinematics and dynamics of parallel and legged mechanisms
  • Perform workspace, singularity, and manipulability analysis
  • Design and evaluate gait patterns and stability criteria for multi-legged robots
  • Simulate and validate legged robot behavior through a full design project
RBE 550

Motion Planning

Motion planning algorithms and their applications to manipulators, mobile robots, and humanoid robots. Topics include search algorithms, combinatorial and sampling-based planners (RRT, PRM), collision detection and avoidance, and planning for non-holonomic and kinodynamic systems.

Learning outcomes
  • Implement and compare search-based and sampling-based planning algorithms
  • Apply RRT*, PRM, and probabilistic methods to high-dimensional configuration spaces
  • Handle collision detection, avoidance constraints, and kinodynamic feasibility
  • Plan trajectories for manipulator arms and mobile robots in ROS/C++/Python
RBE 502

Robot Controls

Control theory with focus on robotic manipulators and mobile robots. Covers feedback control, adaptive control, and the interplay between control design and robot dynamics. Applications include joint-space and task-space control, trajectory tracking, and stability analysis.

Learning outcomes
  • Design and analyze feedback controllers for robotic arms and mobile robots
  • Apply adaptive and robust control strategies to uncertain systems
  • Implement trajectory tracking with PID and model-based controllers
  • Assess stability using Lyapunov methods and frequency-domain tools
RBE 501

Robot Dynamics

Dynamics of robotic manipulators and mechanisms. Topics include computational models of objects and motion, forward and inverse dynamics, Lagrangian and Newton-Euler formulations, dynamic simulation, and dynamical control. Applications to arms and mobile robots.

Learning outcomes
  • Derive equations of motion for multi-body robotic systems
  • Apply Lagrangian and Newton-Euler methods to arm and legged robot dynamics
  • Implement dynamic simulation and validate against physical behavior
  • Use dynamic models to inform control and motion planning design
RBE 500

Foundations of Robotics

Core graduate survey of robotics engineering. Topics span spatial kinematics, control systems, probabilistic robotics, and robot ethics. Establishes the mathematical and systems foundations for all subsequent graduate coursework.

Learning outcomes
  • Represent and transform spatial poses using homogeneous coordinates and Lie groups
  • Apply probabilistic methods to state estimation and localization
  • Analyze robotic systems from sensing through actuation
  • Reason about ethical and social dimensions of robotic deployment

MQP

Major Qualifying Project — SMAC 6.0

WPI's capstone project equivalent. Symbiotic Multi-Agent Construction 6.0: a decentralized multi-robot construction system in which two 5-DOF inchworm robots collaboratively build structures using stigmergy — embedding build-state information in smart blocks rather than a central planner.

Learning outcomes

  • Built dynamic trajectory planning and modular moveset algorithms for two builders
  • Integrated smart block microcontroller mesh for peer-to-peer structure-state communication
  • Executed full hardware/software system integration from simulation to physical deployment

RBE 4815

Industrial Robotics

Industrial serial arm robots (spherical wrist) and parallel manipulators (Stewart-Gough, Delta). Degrees of freedom, inverse and forward kinematics (position and velocity), workspace, singularity, manipulability analysis, end effectors, motion accuracy, and robot control for automation. Hands-on industrial robot lab.

Learning outcomes
  • Analyze and program industrial serial and parallel robotic manipulators
  • Solve forward and inverse kinematic problems for complex wrist configurations
  • Evaluate workspace limits, singularities, and manipulability of industrial arms
  • Deploy and validate robot programs on physical industrial hardware
RBE 4701

Artificial Intelligence for Robotics

AI techniques applied to robotics perception, planning, and actuation. Topics include informed and uninformed search, adversarial search, reasoning under uncertainty, reinforcement learning, and deep learning. Labs culminate in a final project on perception and navigation in a dynamic environment.

Learning outcomes
  • Implement informed search (A*), uninformed search (BFS/DFS), and adversarial search
  • Apply probabilistic reasoning and Bayesian methods to sensor fusion and localization
  • Design and train reinforcement learning agents for robotic decision-making
  • Integrate deep learning models into a perception-to-navigation pipeline
