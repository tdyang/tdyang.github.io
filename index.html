Multi-robot · Swarm · Embedded 2025

SMAC 6.0 — Symbiotic Multi-Agent Construction

Two 5-DOF inchworm robots autonomously pick and place smart blocks to build structures in tandem using stigmergy — information embedded in the blocks themselves drives decentralized decision-making without a central controller. My contributions spanned trajectory planning, modular moveset design, and system integration.

  • Decentralized two-builder coordination via stigmergy
  • Dynamic trajectory planning and modular inchworm moveset design
  • Smart blocks with 6-port microcontroller mesh for structure-state communication
  • Advisors: Carlo Pinciroli (RBE/CS), Gregory Lewin (RBE/ME), Xinming Huang (ECE)
